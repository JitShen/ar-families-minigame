AR Families Minigame

Augmented reality quizzes about the Dutch families who settled in Melaka over the centuries.

How to play

  1. Open the family's marker on another screen, or print it.
  2. On your phone, tap Play and allow camera access.
  3. Point the camera at the marker. The quiz appears on top of it.
  4. Tap an answer. Green is correct, red is wrong.
